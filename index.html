Use three well-spaced, non-collinear landmarks or grid intersections, ideally around the route. Enter their independently verified WGS84 latitude, longitude. Photo GPS alone cannot calibrate a map.

0 of 3 image locations marked. Wrong coordinates or projection will produce the wrong GPX.

2. Extract the visible line

Mark at least two points in hiking order. Add via points on every intended branch. For a loop, add via points and finish at the start.

Lower values isolate the route more strictly. Increase only if the line varies in colour. Black, white, dashed, obscured or disconnected routes need a clearer source image.

    Built with care

    Know what’s behind
    your route.

    Clear limits are part of the product.
    Here’s what this first release can—and cannot—do.

    What works in this preview?

    Paste decimal coordinates, import TXT/CSV or GPX, paste a route screenshot, review photo GPS, and place map pins. Calibrated coloured map lines can be extracted as unverified image references. Reverse the current direction and save PNG or GPX; source track segments and their gaps are preserved. Coordinate-only input stays as separate waypoints. Nothing is snapped or repaired automatically.

    What must happen before route generation?

    We need regional government trail sources, access and closure information, real path-network routing, and dated public-transport checks. The gpx-trail-fix workflow requires real OpenStreetMap geometry, trail-relation checks, honest elevation statistics, and transit caveats. Its Python route repair is not running inside this page. Hiking relations alone do not establish government management.

    Distance, ascent, travel time, and safety scores are intentionally absent until they can be supported. Unknown conditions must not silently pass a safety check.

    How are map photos handled?

    Images are processed locally and never sent to an AI provider. “Paste a route map → GPX” accepts a flat screenshot, three known coordinate references, and a selected continuous route colour. It extracts adjacent matching pixels in the order you mark, then requires a visual review. The converter does not automatically identify an unknown region, correct angled photos, bridge gaps, or verify trails. A wrong projection, calibration or same-colour branch can still produce a wrong result. GPS in a photo may describe the camera location and is never used to locate the depicted map.

    Where does my data go?

    This app does not upload your files or store your draft between visits. Google Analytics loads only if you allow optional analytics. It measures page views and fixed app-action names, plus standard browser/device and approximate location data. We do not send coordinates, filenames, photos, GPX contents, typed text, query strings, or referrer URLs to Analytics. Automatic enhanced measurement and advertising features are disabled. You can withdraw consent using “Privacy settings” below. Browser privacy signals and blockers may prevent tracking.

    Your analytics choice is stored locally; analytics cookies, if accepted, are limited to this project. GitHub hosts the page; Google Fonts and the pinned Leaflet and Exifr libraries load from external providers. OpenStreetMap receives requests for the map tiles you view, which reveal the approximate area and your IP address. PNG export uses tiles already displayed; it does not download an offline map.
